The Second World War at sea ran from September 1939 to August 1945. It started as a war of raiders and convoys in the Atlantic, grew into carrier battles across the Pacific, and ended with the battleship no longer deciding anything.

## The Atlantic and the Arctic

Germany opened the war with raiders. The pocket battleship Admiral Graf Spee sank nine British merchant ships without killing a single sailor, then met three British cruisers off the River Plate in December 1939. Her captain [scuttled her in front of Montevideo](/stories/graf-spee-river-plate/), convinced by a fleet that was not there. Her sister was [renamed Lutzow because Hitler feared the headline "Deutschland sunk"](/stories/deutschland-renamed/).

In June 1940 the carrier HMS Glorious was [caught by Scharnhorst and Gneisenau with no aircraft in the air](/stories/hms-glorious-doyly-hughes/), and 1,519 British sailors died. In May 1941 Bismarck sank HMS Hood in the Denmark Strait. [Three men out of 1,418 survived Hood](/stories/hms-hood-three-survivors/). Days later [a Swordfish biplane jammed Bismarck's rudder](/stories/bismarck-last-battle/) and the Home Fleet caught her. Her sister [Tirpitz spent the war in Norwegian fjords](/stories/tirpitz-lone-queen/) and still tied down British heavy ships for three years. To keep her out of the Atlantic, the Royal Navy [rammed an explosive-packed destroyer into the St Nazaire drydock](/stories/hms-campbeltown-st-nazaire/) in 1942.

The Arctic convoys to Murmansk carried war supplies to the Soviet Union. HMS Edinburgh [sank on the homeward run with 465 gold bars in her bomb room](/stories/hms-edinburgh-gold/). On New Year's Eve 1942, [six destroyers held off a pocket battleship and a heavy cruiser in the Barents Sea](/stories/barents-sea-new-years-eve/), and Hitler turned on his surface fleet. In the Mediterranean, Force H shelled Genoa in February 1941, and [one British shell went through the cathedral roof without exploding](/stories/genoa-cathedral-shell/).

## The Pacific carrier war

Japan's attack on Pearl Harbor on 7 December 1941 brought the United States into the war. [USS Arizona lost 1,177 men](/stories/uss-arizona-pearl-harbor/), including her band. [USS Nevada was the only battleship to get underway](/stories/uss-nevada-pearl-harbor/). [USS New Orleans, with no power, passed shells to her guns by hand](/stories/uss-new-orleans-pearl-harbor/), and [USS Phoenix escaped untouched](/stories/uss-phoenix-belgrano/), to be sunk in 1982 as Argentina's Belgrano.

At the Java Sea in February 1942, [Karel Doorman led a four-nation force with no common signal code](/stories/karel-doorman-java-sea/) against the invasion fleet. In June, US codebreakers [used a fake water-shortage message to confirm the target was Midway](/stories/battle-of-midway/), and Japan lost four carriers. [USS Enterprise](/stories/uss-enterprise-cv6/) earned 20 battle stars and was announced sunk by Japan three times.

Guadalcanal turned into six months of night fighting. More than fifty warships went down in [Ironbottom Sound](/stories/ironbottom-sound/). One torpedo took 150 feet off USS New Orleans, and her crew [sailed her backwards with a bow of coconut logs](/stories/uss-new-orleans-coconut-bow/), an improvisation in the line of the Royal Navy's 1917 [HMS Zubian](/stories/hms-zubian-frankenship/), welded from two wrecked destroyers. Cruiser design had its own fights. Japan's [Mogami class](/stories/mogami-cardboard-cruiser/) was built over treaty limits. The American [Cleveland class](/stories/cleveland-class-top-heavy/) grew top-heavy under added anti-aircraft guns, and [USS Wichita needed pig iron ballast](/stories/uss-wichita-pig-iron/) to stay upright. In the Atlantic in 1943, the destroyer [USS William D. Porter fired a torpedo at the battleship carrying Roosevelt](/stories/uss-porter-torpedo-fdr/).

## The end of the battleship

At [Leyte Gulf in October 1944](/stories/battle-of-leyte-gulf/), Japan used a carrier force with almost no planes as bait, and escort carriers off Samar faced Yamato alone. A month later [Shinano, the largest carrier yet built, sank seventeen hours into her first voyage](/stories/shinano-maiden-voyage/). In April 1945 [Yamato sailed for Okinawa](/stories/yamato-last-sortie/) and was sunk by aircraft without seeing an American battleship. [HMS Warspite](/stories/hms-warspite-grand-old-lady/), a veteran of Jutland, survived a German guided bomb at Salerno in 1943.

In January 1945 a Soviet submarine sank the refugee ship [Wilhelm Gustloff](/stories/wilhelm-gustloff/), the deadliest sinking ever recorded. In July, [USS Indianapolis delivered the uranium for the first atomic bomb](/stories/uss-indianapolis-sharks/) and was torpedoed on the way back. A year after the war, the US Navy anchored captured ships at Bikini, and [Prinz Eugen survived two atomic bombs before sinking from a leak](/stories/prinz-eugen-bikini/).
