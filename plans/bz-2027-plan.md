## 5. Metrics and monthly checkpoint

Weekly (Noah report, Friday): day-1 backers from the core per live launch (north star), weekly active members, members who shared/invited, % who bought in last 6 months, Launch Crew size, new members by engine.

**Monthly checkpoint (first Friday, 15 min, Max reads one page):**
1. Members and Launch Crew vs section 1 targets: on track / behind.
2. Last launch: day-1 backers total, how many matched to members (email match), vs target in section 1.
3. Engine table: members per Max-hour actual vs section 4. Drop the worst engine if it is below 5/hour for 2 months.
4. Max hours spent vs 2 h/week.
5. One decision for Max (one only).

Kill rule: if two consecutive launches get fewer than half the day-1 core target, stop new channels and run buyer/non-buyer interviews before anything else.

## 6. Riskiest assumption and cheapest test (first 30 days)

**Riskiest assumption:** past MOZUMA backers who opt in to the Launch Crew back the next launch inside 48 hours at 20%+ (that is, a Crew alert creates day-1 backers rather than people who would have bought anyway, later). Every target in section 1 multiplies off this number. If it is 5%, the community needs 4x the size and the plan changes.

**Cheapest test (days 1-30, ~1 h Max):**
1. Days 1-5: Noah + ezra run read-only overlap count: Yamato x Cruisers x Texas x coin reservers. Output: repeat-backer rate. No prod writes.
2. Days 5-10: one Launch Crew opt-in email to past naval backers not on the never-mail list (Max approves text). Measure opt-in rate.
3. Days 10-30: use the next live coin drop or Texas late-pledge/PM step as the test launch: Crew gets a 24h first-access alert. Measure % of Crew who reserve within 48h vs a holdout of non-Crew past backers who get the normal email at the same time.
4. Pass: Crew 48h conversion >= 15% and at least 2x the holdout. Fail: rethink what core members get (first access vs recognition vs a say in what we make), per BRIEF's open question.

Other risks: remembrance/commerce mix-up (rule: no commerce on R dates, Noah checks the calendar before each queue); Big J naming before clearance; AI images or AI stories leaking to BZ (archival photos with credit only); Max time creep above 2 h (monthly checkpoint item 4).
